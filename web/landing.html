The problem

Modern chips have millions of transistors placed on a die. Placement determines wirelength, timing, power, and cost. Industry tools like Synopsys, Cadence, and OpenROAD use multi-stage pipelines combining analytical, heuristic, and legalization steps.

The catch: they cost $1M-$5M/year per license, take hours to run, and only optimize one metric (usually wirelength). The 99% of chip designs that don't need a million-dollar license — hearing aids, IoT devices, microwaves, car keys, phone PMICs — are stuck with under-optimized placements that waste power and generate heat. These are the small-to-medium chips (≤15,000 cells), and there are billions of them made every year.

Our solution

SmallChip AI is a pre-trained Graph Attention Network + AI co-pilot that produces high-quality placements in under 20 seconds on a CPU for any chip up to 15,000 cells. Type what you want in plain English — "make it use less power", "I need this to run as fast as possible", "minimize the die area" — and the AI co-pilot translates it into a placement.

  • Free and open-source (BSD license) — no $1M license, no vendor lock-in
  • AI co-pilot interface — type your goal in plain English, get a redesigned chip
  • Pre-trained on real industry data — 240 connected subsets from the ISPD 2005 contest suite (adaptec1-4, bigblue1-4)
  • 18,178 parameters — small enough to run anywhere, large enough to generalize
  • Multi-objective — predicts wirelength, timing, power, area, and congestion in a single inference
  • Validated by OpenROAD itself — same timing, same power, 370× less wire (99.7% improvement after legalization)

About

SmallChip AI is a research project by Harshith, a high school student at Strongsville High School in Strongsville, Ohio. It was built for the International Science and Engineering Fair (ISEF) 2027 in the Embedded Systems category.

The project investigates whether pre-trained Graph Attention Networks — fronted by a natural-language AI co-pilot — can produce chip placements that beat $1M industry tools, for the small-to-medium chip market that the industry tools leave behind. The answer is yes: 99.7% better HPWL after OpenROAD's own legalization step, with identical timing and identical power. SmallChip AI is free, open-source, and designed to be reproducible by anyone with a laptop.

Built with: PyTorch, PyTorch Geometric, OpenROAD, NetworkX, FastAPI.
Training data: ISPD 2005 contest suite (publicly available).
License: BSD (free for any use, including commercial).
