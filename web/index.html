What these metrics mean

  • HPWL — Wirelength. Lower = cheaper to manufacture, faster signals.
  • Timing — Estimated critical path delay. Lower = faster chip.
  • Power — Estimated dynamic power. Lower = better battery, less heat.
  • Area — Silicon area used. Lower = smaller chip, lower cost.
  • Congestion — Max routing demand. Lower = easier to manufacture.

About SmallChip AI

SmallChip AI is the first free, open-source, AI-powered chip placement tool for the small-to-medium chip market (≤15,000 cells). It predicts 5 quality metrics simultaneously and ships with a natural-language AI co-pilot that lets you describe what you want in plain English. Most industry tools (Synopsys, Cadence) only optimize wirelength. They cost millions and take hours.

We pre-trained Graph Attention Networks on real industry benchmarks (ISPD 2005: adaptec, bigblue series) and built small ML models to predict timing, power, area, and congestion from netlist features.

Why this matters: Most chips are in microwaves, TVs, hearing aids, car keys, phone PMICs, IoT sensors. They're small (under 15K cells) but there are billions of them. A free, fast, multi-objective tool helps the 99% of chip designs that don't need a $1M license.
